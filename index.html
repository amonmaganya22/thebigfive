The Big Five Spotlight

Originally coined by big-game hunters, the "Big Five" represents the five most majestic and formidable iconic animals of the African savanna.

King of Serengeti

African Lion

Renowned for their majestic manes and powerful roars echoing across Ngorongoro Crater and Serengeti plains.

Sightability: High
Silent Master

African Leopard

Elusive and stealthy hunters frequently spotted lounging along acacia branches in Tarangire & Lake Manyara.

Sightability: Rare
Gentle Giant

African Elephant

Earth's largest land mammal. Roam in massive herds under the backdrop of Mount Kilimanjaro.

Sightability: Very High
Protected Legend

Black Rhino

Critically endangered and heavily guarded. Best protected sanctuary viewings are in Ngorongoro Crater floor.

Sightability: Exclusive
Savanna Warrior

Cape Buffalo

Fearless and protective herd creatures with distinctive hooked horns, essential to savanna biodiversity.

Sightability: High

Serengeti & Ngorongoro Crater Expedition

5 Days / 4 Nights Itinerary

  • Day 1: Arusha to Tarangire National Park - Game drive & lodge check-in.
  • Day 2: Tarangire to Central Serengeti - Endless plains and big cat tracking.
  • Day 3: Full Day Serengeti Game Drive - Wildebeest migration searching.
  • Day 4: Serengeti to Ngorongoro Crater Rim - Evening caldera sunset view.
  • Day 5: Ngorongoro Crater Floor Safari - Return to Arusha for departure.

Mount Kilimanjaro Summit Trek

7 Days / 6 Nights (Machame Route)

  • Day 1: Machame Gate to Machame Camp (3,000m).
  • Day 2: Machame Camp to Shira Camp (3,840m).
  • Day 3: Shira Camp via Lava Tower (4,630m) to Barranco Camp (3,960m).
  • Day 4: Barranco Camp over Barranco Wall to Karanga Camp (3,963m).
  • Day 5: Karanga Camp to Barafu Camp (4,600m).
  • Day 6: Summit Night (Uhuru Peak 5,895m) then descend to Mweka Camp.
  • Day 7: Mweka Camp to Mweka Gate & Transfer to Hotel.

Luxury Wildlife & Photo Safari
